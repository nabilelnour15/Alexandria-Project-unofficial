1 · Data check: fix first

These are the problems that make the site look official, out of date or invented. The fact-checker verified them against web sources.

Fixed

The site looked like an official government site

The footer shows info-unofficial@alexandria.gov.eg (the real government domain), "Governorate Building", +20 3 XXX XXXX and "© Alexandria. All rights reserved."Use your own contact details and the line "Fan project, not affiliated with the Alexandria Governorate".
Fixed

Quotes were fabricated and attributed to real people

The Governor (newsData.ts:19), BA Director Dr. Ahmed Zayed (:55), the head of the Transport Authority (:37), and an "Official Slogan" label (GovernorPage.tsx:387).Remove them, or replace them with sourced quotes that link to where they came from.
Fixed

The governor was out of date

In the February 2026 rotation, Ayman Mohamed Ibrahim Attia replaced Vice-Admiral Ahmed Khaled. He was sworn in on 16 Feb 2026 (EgyptToday).Rewrite the page for the new governor, or present the current one as "Governor 2024–2026".
Resolved

The news articles were fictional (news section removed)

  • The festival article says "40th edition", but the 41st ran on 2–6 Oct 2025.
  • The tech summit appears to be invented.
  • The museum reopening is dated 2025, but it was 2023.
  • The bylines look invented.
  • The home page news section hard-codes 5 different items that don't link anywhere.
Label every article "Illustrative sample", or replace them with real linked news. Build the home page section from newsData.
High

Factual errors

  • "Two international airports": El Nouzha has been closed since 2011–12.
  • "Oldest electric tram (1863)": it was horse-drawn until it was electrified in 1902.
  • Electric buses: the first ones arrived in 2018, not 2023. The fleet is given as 15 in one file and 55 in another.
  • The Raml tram is blue and cream; the yellow trams are the city lines.
  • Train fares are given in USD, but tickets are in EGP and cost a few dollars.

2 · Bugs, usability & code

High

Inner pages break when refreshed

base: './' together with BrowserRouter means refreshing /news/1 shows a blank page. There is also no 404 page.Set base: '/', add a rewrite rule on the host, and add <Route path="*">.
High

Buttons that do nothing

Plan Your Visit, Read More, Explore Details, Share, the tag chips, the search, the newsletter and the EN button.Connect each one or remove it.
High

Slow first load

A fake 2-second loader, all the code in one 1.07 MB file, and 28 MB of images. One image is 3.5 MB, and 8 images are never used.Remove the loader, load each page's code only when it's opened, convert images to WebP/AVIF at 1600 px or less, and lazy-load them.
High

Accessibility

Animations ignore the visitor's reduce-motion setting. The looping typing animation in the <h1> keeps being re-read by screen readers. Some cards are clickable divs that a keyboard can't reach, and the popups don't behave as proper dialogs.
High

SEO and sharing previews

Only a <title>: no description, OpenGraph image or favicon, and every page shares one title.
Medium

Friction and structure

The disclaimer pops up on every visit (show a popup once, then a banner). Faint text fails the contrast ratio. There are two <h1>s, a <main> inside another <main>, no skip link, and no news filters.

Code health

  • Build passes. Lint has 8 errors, all in the generated components/ui files.
  • 9 pages each repeat the navbar and footer. Use one layout route with <Outlet/> instead.
  • 413 colour codes are typed directly into 17 files. Move them into shared colour variables.
  • Unused code: 5 sections, ServicesPage, App.css, about 52 shadcn UI files and about 15 packages.
  • PlaceholderImage has been copied 3 times, and the same scroll-reveal code is repeated in 7 sections.
  • kimi-plugin-inspect-react runs in the live build. @tailwindcss/typography is missing, so the prose classes do nothing.

4 · Future design: the judges' best picks

1

"Honest Ledger" trust layer

Every figure carries a source, an "as of" date and a label: Official, Reported or Estimate. Concept renders get a "Concept" badge, and each page shows "Last reviewed" and "Report an inaccuracy". All figures come from one facts.ts file, so the same fact can't have two values.

Effort S–M10/10 from all three judges
2

"Pharos to Future" timeline

One scrolling timeline from 331 BC to 2030: Ptolemaic, Roman, Coptic, Jewish community, Arab conquest (641), Mamluk/Qaitbay, the cosmopolitan era and the 1863 tram, the Bibliotheca in 2002, then current projects. A light beam fills as you scroll, and future items are dashed and marked "planned".

Effort MThe single bridge between heritage and future
3

Honest projects dashboard

Project status filters, a chart of who funds each project (EIB / AFD / EU / Government), a gauge of committed vs identified vs unfunded money, and Vision 2030 goals linked to their projects.

Effort S–MUses existing data · replaces the made-up €506M
4

Layered city map

One SVG map with the ancient coastline and Heptastadion, today's Corniche, and planned routes marked "indicative location".

Effort LCurator 9/10Do it later
5

Calls to action that fit each page

Projects: "Follow progress". Home: "See Alexandria 2030". Only once the buttons actually work.
